# 第 02 部分：问题数据与最终文本

## 本部分目标

把三张流程图中的问题和选项录入 `WBP_CycleReflection`，保持固定顺序。所有普通选项无分支地进入下一题。

## 1. 在 Widget 中创建问题数组

打开：

```text
WBP_CycleReflection
```

添加变量：

```text
Questions : S_CycleReflectionQuestion Array
```

设为 Private 即可，不需要 Instance Editable，不写入 Game Instance。

在 Class Defaults 中依次添加 5 个元素。

## 2. Question 01

```text
QuestionID = Reflection_01
QuestionText = Welcome back.
Each time you returned, did you gain new knowledge or experience?
```

Options：

```text
YES
NO
NOT SURE
```

第一题的 `Welcome back.` 与问题正文之间保留一次手动换行。简短选项可以与后续较长的英文陈述形成刻意的“初始判断 / 深入陈述”节奏差异。

## 3. Question 02

```text
QuestionID = Reflection_02
QuestionText = Have you seen what lies beneath the sea?
```

Options：

```text
I have no idea what you are referring to.

Yes, I have, but it is far from the only secret. This palace still conceals histories waiting to be uncovered.

Yes, I have. It is both truth and lie. This palace exists upon such a shared belief.
```

## 4. Question 03

```text
QuestionID = Reflection_03
QuestionText = Did you try to interpret the meaning of the named objects?
```

Options：

```text
Yes I did. I believe the world is ultimately built upon order and rules. Once I understand how things are connected, I can decide what shape the world takes.

Well… perhaps they signify something, but I do not believe the system needs to be explained. All I need to do is keep moving forward.

None of it matters. The meaning of life lies in trusting my instincts. Wherever they lead me, there I find my spiritual home.
```

## 5. Question 04

```text
QuestionID = Reflection_04
QuestionText = When you make a choice, do you believe it is a moment that determines your fate?
```

Options：

```text
Yes. The choices I make according to my convictions are real and meaningful, and I shall take responsibility for whatever consequences may follow. Through those choices, I form a profound connection with this world.

The world is fundamentally absurd and without order. What we call fate is only an accumulation of random events. But people need an anchor for their existence, and so the power to choose becomes proof of freedom.

I have never thought about it. Game is for fun, right? Why take it so seriously?
```

## 6. Question 05

```text
QuestionID = Reflection_05
QuestionText = Does Osaider truly exist? Have you ever believed the cycle had meaning?
```

Options：

```text
To be honest, I still do not fully understand any of this. I need to go back and find more clues.

I am exhausted by this endless search for meaning and have begun to think that truth does not exist. This palace has retold its stories too many times, yet it has never allowed me to see it whole. It remains a mysterious apparition, ignoring the wandering souls within it.

Whether Osaider exists is not the real question. Osaider is a constructed concept. What we should be asking is why it was created. I kept searching for this named enemy, but it was this nameless palace itself that shaped me.
```

这里必须保持当前世界观：

- 宫殿没有名字；
- Osaider 只是假想敌的名字；
- 被命名的威胁吸引注意力；
- 无名的宫殿作为既定现实塑造玩家。

## 7. 最终决定文本

在 `WBP_CycleReflection` 添加 Text 变量：

```text
FinalDecisionText
```

内容：

```text
Now it is time to make your final decision.
Will you return to the cycle built by the Archivist, or follow the Whalemen and break the cycle?

But I must warn you: if you leave now, you will lose everything you have gained—including the palace itself. It will be forgotten forever.
```

最终按钮：

```text
I want to return.
I want to leave.
```

按钮下方可使用较小的功能说明：

```text
RETURN: Begin the cycle again (Prototype_07)
LEAVE: Exit the cycle (return to L_Intro)
```

正式版本如果希望保持沉浸，可隐藏括号内的关卡名，仅在开发版本显示。

## 8. 数据无后果规则

普通答案 OnClicked 后不要保存 Answer Value。

推荐流程只有：

```text
OptionIndex
→ 播放本地选中反馈
→ 暂停接受输入
→ 播放题目退出动画
→ CurrentQuestionIndex + 1
→ 显示下一题
```

即使为了 Debug 临时 Print `QuestionID` 和 `OptionIndex`，也不要把它写入持久变量或存档。

## 9. 文本排版注意

- 英文正文启用自动换行；
- 不手动在每行插入换行符；
- 只在段落边界保留空行；
- `Osaider`、`Archivist`、`Whalemen` 保持英文拼写；
- 选项不加 A/B/C，避免产生考试感；
- 不显示选项对应的红蓝颜色。

## 10. 本部分验收

- [ ] Questions 数组长度为 5。
- [ ] 顺序与流程图一致。
- [ ] 每题有 3 个选项。
- [ ] 第 5 题明确区分无名宫殿与被命名的 Osaider。
- [ ] 最终决定有 2 个按钮。
- [ ] 普通答案没有 Gameplay 字段与持久化逻辑。
